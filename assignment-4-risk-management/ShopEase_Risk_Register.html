Expected monetary value and contingency

EMV = probability x cost impact. Threats are positive, opportunities negative. The net EMV is compared with the contingency reserve in the cost baseline.

EMV by risk

Red bars: threats (cost). Blue bars: opportunities (saving). Grey outline: after response.

Monte Carlo simulation of go-live date and cost

Each run samples every activity duration from its three-point (PERT) estimate, lets each risk occur with its probability (delay or gain in its activity, cost added or saved), and recalculates the network with all FS/SS/FF links and lags.
